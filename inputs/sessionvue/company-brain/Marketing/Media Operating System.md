## Idea → content (stage map)

Stages reference Jobs; they do not replace Job definitions.

1. **Priority** → Executive Job: Prioritize Projects  
2. **Plan** → Marketing Job: Plan and Produce Campaign  
3. **Produce** → Marketing Job: Produce Content (+ Automation: Content Drafting Pipeline when activated)  
4. **Approve** → Marketing Job: Route Publishing Approval (founder decides)  
5. **Publish** → Marketing Job: Publish Approved Content (handoff; platform execution may be external — Art. IX)  
6. **Demand** → Marketing Job: Generate Qualified Demand  
7. **Review** → Executive Job: Prepare Founder Brief (+ Funnel and Demand Report / Qualified Demand Generated)  
8. **Improve** → Marketing Job: Capture Marketing Improvement
