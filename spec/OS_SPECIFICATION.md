# SessionVue Operating System (SVOS)
## Operating System Specification
Version: 1.0.0
Status: LOCKED
Authority: Founder

---

# Purpose

SVOS (SessionVue Operating System) is the canonical operating system used to design, build, execute, monitor, and continuously improve SessionVue.

SVOS does not run the application.

SVOS runs the company.

The company is treated as a deterministic operating system composed of knowledge, systems, projects, jobs, workflows, automations, and decisions.

Everything inside SessionVue must compile from this specification.

Nothing below this specification may redefine architecture.

---

# Core Principle

The company is not built manually.

The company is compiled.

Every department, project, workflow, job, automation, registry, report, and dashboard is generated from standards defined by SVOS Core.

---

# Architecture

SVOS is divided into seven layers.

Layer 0
SVOS Core

Defines architecture.

Never references generated systems.

Never references projects.

Changes extremely rarely.

---

Layer 1

Company Brain

Defines company knowledge.

Mission

Vision

Brand

Products

Memberships

Customers

Pricing

Integrations

Objects

Facts

Never executes work.

---

Layer 2

Business Systems

Permanent operating departments.

Executive

Operations

Marketing

Product

Engineering

Analytics

Sales

Finance

Customer Success

Support

Legal

People

These own HOW work is performed.

---

Layer 3

Projects

Temporary initiatives.

Projects borrow Business Systems.

Projects never own architecture.

Projects complete.

Projects archive.

---

Layer 4

Jobs

Reusable execution units.

Jobs belong to Business Systems.

Projects activate Jobs.

Jobs never own Projects.

---

Layer 5

Automation

Automation executes Jobs.

Automation never changes architecture.

Automation never becomes source of truth.

---

Layer 6

Continuous Improvement

Every execution creates learning.

Learning improves standards.

Standards regenerate systems.

---

# Canonical Rule

Every object has exactly one source of truth.

Knowledge is never duplicated.

If duplicate knowledge exists

SVOS must reject compilation.

---

# Generated Rule

Business Systems are generated.

Projects are generated.

Jobs are generated.

Registries are generated.

Indexes are generated.

Dashboards are generated.

Validation reports are generated.

Nothing generated should become manually maintained.
