# SVOS Relationship Model

Business Systems own Jobs.

Projects activate Jobs.

Jobs execute Workflows.

Workflows invoke Automations.

Automations produce Outputs.

Outputs generate Metrics.

Metrics inform Decisions.

Decisions improve Business Systems.

Business Systems regenerate Projects.

The loop never ends.

---

Knowledge flows downward.

Execution flows upward.
