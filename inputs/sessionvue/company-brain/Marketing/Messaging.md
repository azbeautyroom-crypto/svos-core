# Messaging

## One-sentence thesis (approved)

The best beauty professionals are the hardest to find — not because they market badly, but because
the tools they've been given were built for browsing, not discovery. SessionVue is the world built
for discovery.

## The argument, in order

1. **Reframe:** a discovery issue, not a skills issue. The system failed her; she did not fail.
2. **Mechanism:** a client searches for a fit — service + specialty + city + price + availability;
   discovery connects a specific need to a specific professional.
3. **World:** SessionVue is not a tool she learns — it is a world she steps into.
4. **Honest ask:** join the Professional Waitlist — **belief, not urgency.**
